Dot QR

Type a link, draw a small black-and-white picture (or start from a photo), and get a QR code with your drawing sitting in the middle — untouched, and still scannable. Everything runs in your browser.

Drawing

Arrow keys move the cursor; Space or Enter turns that dot on or off.

Start from

Click or drag to draw; switch to Erase (or right-drag) to remove dots. Only what you draw goes into the code — empty space around it is cropped away.

QR code

Paste or type a link above to make your QR code.

Grid

The dots replace the drawing on the grid as a starting point — keep drawing and erasing on top, or undo. Your photo never leaves this device.

Tips

  • Shorter links fit bigger drawings: a link of about 30 characters holds a full 32 × 32 drawing. A link shortener helps.
  • Photos work best cropped tight around one simple subject.
  • Before printing, scan the download with your phone. Print it at least 4 cm (1.6 in) wide and keep the white border.

The trick behind it comes from Russ Cox’s QArt Codes.
